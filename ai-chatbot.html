Case Study

Sanki Events

Intelligent WhatsApp Automation & Lead Generation Engine

Problem Statement

The Bottleneck in Customer Communication

Sanki Events, a premier event management company, was facing a massive influx of daily customer inquiries via social media and messaging platforms. Potential clients were asking repetitive questions regarding event packages, venue availability, and base pricing.

Because their sales team had to manually reply to every message, response times were lagging. This delay caused a significant drop-off in high-intent leads who expected instant answers. Sanki Events needed an automated, conversational system capable of qualifying leads instantly and providing 24/7 customer engagement without sounding robotic.

Our Solution

NLP-Driven WhatsApp Lead Engine

Dynamic Labz engineered a sophisticated conversational AI bot integrated directly into the WhatsApp Business API. Utilizing Natural Language Processing (NLP), the system goes beyond rigid button-menus; it accurately understands user intent, allowing clients to type inquiries naturally.

When a user initiates a chat, the bot automatically guides them through a conversational lead-qualification flow—asking for their event type, guest count, and estimated budget. It instantly delivers dynamic PDF brochures tailored to their answers. If the inquiry requires human intervention, the bot seamlessly hands the conversation over to a live sales agent, simultaneously pushing the captured lead data directly into Sanki Events' CRM.

Key Features

  • NLP Intent Recognition: Processes natural language to understand exactly what the user is asking.
  • 24/7 Automated Engagement: Zero downtime, ensuring inquiries are answered instantly.
  • Multi-format Media Support: Instantly sends images, locations, and PDF brochures.
  • Smart CRM Integration: Pushes qualified lead data straight to the sales dashboard.
  • Seamless Human Handoff: Pauses the bot automatically when a user requests to speak to a human agent.

Results

Maximizing Sales Funnel Efficiency

90% Faster Response Time

Reduced initial inquiry response time from hours down to milliseconds.

300% Increase in Lead Capture

24/7 availability ensured night-time and weekend leads were captured immediately.

Massive Time Savings

Saved the sales team over 40 hours a week by eliminating repetitive manual replies.
