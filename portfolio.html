AI Document Validation

Enterprise AI Automation System

Problem Statement

Manual Validation Challenges in MedTech

A leading MedTech firm in the UK was facing significant inefficiencies in processing and validating crucial data from 25+ police forces. Their workflow required manually reviewing PDF and DOCX files containing essential field information, ensuring compliance and accuracy across 15+ key parameters. This process was time-consuming, prone to human error, and lacked scalability.

With hundreds of documents arriving daily, their team struggled to maintain speed and accuracy. Delays in validation impacted decision-making and operational efficiency, while errors led to compliance risks. The company needed an intelligent, automated solution to streamline data validation, reduce turnaround time, and enhance precision.

Our Solution

AI-Powered Document Validation System

To tackle the inefficiencies in manual validation, we developed a state-of-the-art AI-powered document validation system designed specifically for the MedTech firm. This intelligent system automates the extraction, analysis, and validation of key fields from incoming documents, ensuring unmatched accuracy and efficiency. Our AI model was meticulously trained on real-world police reports to recognize diverse formats, handle inconsistencies, and validate critical data points seamlessly.

The system operates in three core stages: Document Parsing, AI-Driven Validation, and Structured Output Generation. First, incoming PDFs and DOCX files from 25+ police forces are scanned and processed using advanced OCR and NLP techniques. Once extracted, each of the 15+ key fields undergoes rigorous validation using AI algorithms that detect missing or incomplete information. The final output is then converted into a structured JSON format, enabling seamless integration with the client's existing infrastructure.

SECTION 02

Automated field validation ensures all required data is complete.

SECTION 03

Sanki Events

Intelligent WhatsApp Automation & Lead Generation Engine

Problem Statement

The Bottleneck in Customer Communication

Sanki Events, a premier event management company, was facing a massive influx of daily customer inquiries via social media and messaging platforms. Potential clients were asking repetitive questions regarding event packages, venue availability, and base pricing.

Because their sales team had to manually reply to every message, response times were lagging. This delay caused a significant drop-off in high-intent leads who expected instant answers. Sanki Events needed an automated, conversational system capable of qualifying leads instantly and providing 24/7 customer engagement without sounding robotic.

Our Solution

NLP-Driven WhatsApp Lead Engine

Dynamic Labz engineered a sophisticated conversational AI bot integrated directly into the WhatsApp Business API. Utilizing Natural Language Processing (NLP), the system goes beyond rigid button-menus; it accurately understands user intent, allowing clients to type inquiries naturally.

When a user initiates a chat, the bot automatically guides them through a conversational lead-qualification flow—asking for their event type, guest count, and estimated budget. It instantly delivers dynamic PDF brochures tailored to their answers. If the inquiry requires human intervention, the bot seamlessly hands the conversation over to a live sales agent, simultaneously pushing the captured lead data directly into Sanki Events' CRM.

Automated Lead Capture

SECTION 02

Dynamic Media Delivery

Automated dispatch of pricing catalogs, venue images, and PDFs directly within the WhatsApp chat.

SECTION 03

Live Agent Handoff & CRM

Key Features

  • NLP Intent Recognition: Processes natural language to understand exactly what the user is asking.
  • 24/7 Automated Engagement: Zero downtime, ensuring inquiries are answered instantly, even after hours.
  • Multi-format Media Support: Instantly sends images, locations, and PDF brochures based on user input.
  • Smart CRM Integration: Pushes qualified lead data (Name, Number, Event Type, Budget) straight to the sales dashboard.
  • Seamless Human Handoff: Pauses the bot automatically when a user requests to speak to a human agent.
