Case Study

Sanki Events

Intelligent WhatsApp Automation & Lead Generation Engine

Problem Statement

The Bottleneck in Customer Communication

Sanki Events, a premier event management company, was facing a massive influx of daily customer inquiries via social media and messaging platforms. Potential clients were asking repetitive questions regarding event packages, venue availability, and base pricing.

Because their sales team had to manually reply to every message, response times were lagging. This delay caused a significant drop-off in high-intent leads who expected instant answers. Sanki Events needed an automated, conversational system capable of qualifying leads instantly and providing 24/7 customer engagement without sounding robotic.

Our Solution

NLP-Driven WhatsApp Lead Engine

Dynamic Labz engineered a sophisticated conversational AI bot integrated directly into the WhatsApp Business API. Utilizing Natural Language Processing (NLP), the system goes beyond rigid button-menus; it accurately understands user intent, allowing clients to type inquiries naturally.

When a user initiates a chat, the bot automatically guides them through a conversational lead-qualification flow—asking for their event type, guest count, and estimated budget. It instantly delivers dynamic PDF brochures tailored to their answers. If the inquiry requires human intervention, the bot seamlessly hands the conversation over to a live sales agent, simultaneously pushing the captured lead data directly into Sanki Events' CRM.

Key Features

  • NLP Intent Recognition: Processes natural language to understand exactly what the user is asking.
  • 24/7 Automated Engagement: Zero downtime, ensuring inquiries are answered instantly.
  • Multi-format Media Support: Instantly sends images, locations, and PDF brochures.
  • Smart CRM Integration: Pushes qualified lead data straight to the sales dashboard.
  • Seamless Human Handoff: Pauses the bot automatically when a user requests to speak to a human agent.

Results

Maximizing Sales Funnel Efficiency

90% Faster Response Time

Reduced initial inquiry response time from hours down to milliseconds.

300% Increase in Lead Capture

24/7 availability ensured night-time and weekend leads were captured immediately.

Massive Time Savings

Saved the sales team over 40 hours a week by eliminating repetitive manual replies.

"Implementing WhatsApp Automation through Dynamic Labz has enhanced our customer communication tremendously. Fast, reliable, and effective. A must-have for any business."

Akshat Shah Founder of Sanki Events

The client, a leading property broker, faced significant challenges in managing property recommendations for walk-in clients. Additionally, the manual system made it difficult to plan property visits effectively. Coordinating schedules between clients, property owners, and brokers required extensive back-and-forth communication, causing delays and confusion. The client needed a comprehensive solution to streamline these processes, improve data accuracy, and enhance the overall client experience.

Our Solution

Property Listing & Visit Planning Portal

We developed an in-depth property listing portal tailored for the client's in-house operations. This portal streamlines the entire property selection process, starting with an extensive client requirement form comprising over 35+ questions. Once submitted, the system applies intelligent filters based on primary criteria—such as identifying properties within a 2.5 km radius.

The portal then presents the top 15 property matches with a visually appealing UI/UX design. Clients can shortlist properties they find interesting, moving seamlessly to the visit planner. Notifications are automatically sent to property owners, ensuring smooth coordination.

The lack of a centralized digital system led to siloed data and inefficient inventory management, which threatened to bottleneck their commitment to impeccable quality and on-time delivery.

Bespoke Manufacturing & Supply Chain ERP

Dynamic Labz engineered a customized Manufacturing & Supply Chain ERP tailored specifically for the construction chemical industry. We integrated their entire production lifecycle—from raw material procurement to final dispatch—into a unified, highly secure dashboard.

The ERP features real-time inventory tracking, automated batch formulation oversight, and a dedicated Quality Control module that ensures every product meets strict specifications before leaving their state-of-the-art laboratory. Furthermore, for their contract manufacturing division, we built an integrated tracking system that securely manages proprietary formulations and B2B order timelines.

Tigray Community Atlanta

Comprehensive Donation & Fund Management ERP

The Goal

Tigray Community Atlanta (TCA) needed a highly secure, centralized system to manage the flow of community capital. Their core requirement was an internal admin ecosystem capable of accurately tracking incoming donations from community members and overseeing the outward distribution of those funds to individuals in need, replacing inefficient manual tracking with absolute transparency.

How We Built It

We engineered a bespoke ERP-style admin panel tailored specifically to non-profit financial logistics. The core of the platform is a robust relational database that captures and categorizes all incoming donation streams.

To ensure granular transparency, we architected a detailed user profile system. Administrators can now instantly pull up individual donor profiles, view lifetime contribution metrics, and track the exact lifecycle of specific transactions—from the moment a donation is logged to its final distribution as relief funding.

The Launch

The deployment of this internal panel brought total operational clarity to TCA's management team. By automating the ledger and housing all transaction histories in one highly accessible, secure dashboard, we drastically reduced administrative overhead and ensured that relief funds could be managed and distributed with complete confidence and accountability.

Benefits

Why this works for Community Organizations

Centralized Dashboard

A real-time, bird's-eye view tracking all incoming donations and outgoing community relief funds in one place.

Comprehensive Profiles

Deep-dive into individual member histories, allowing admins to instantly view lifetime contributions and logs.

Transparent Ledger

A highly secure, easily searchable record of every specific transaction moving through the organization.

Operational Efficiency

Replaces manual spreadsheets with automated data handling, saving hundreds of administrative hours annually.
