Case Study

Sanki Events

Intelligent WhatsApp Automation & Lead Generation Engine

Problem Statement

The Bottleneck in Customer Communication

Sanki Events, a premier event management company, was facing a massive influx of daily customer inquiries via social media and messaging platforms. Potential clients were asking repetitive questions regarding event packages, venue availability, and base pricing.

Because their sales team had to manually reply to every message, response times were lagging. This delay caused a significant drop-off in high-intent leads who expected instant answers. Sanki Events needed an automated, conversational system capable of qualifying leads instantly and providing 24/7 customer engagement without sounding robotic.

Our Solution

NLP-Driven WhatsApp Lead Engine

Dynamic Labz engineered a sophisticated conversational AI bot integrated directly into the WhatsApp Business API. Utilizing Natural Language Processing (NLP), the system goes beyond rigid button-menus; it accurately understands user intent, allowing clients to type inquiries naturally.

When a user initiates a chat, the bot automatically guides them through a conversational lead-qualification flow—asking for their event type, guest count, and estimated budget. It instantly delivers dynamic PDF brochures tailored to their answers. If the inquiry requires human intervention, the bot seamlessly hands the conversation over to a live sales agent, simultaneously pushing the captured lead data directly into Sanki Events' CRM.

Key Features

  • NLP Intent Recognition: Processes natural language to understand exactly what the user is asking.
  • 24/7 Automated Engagement: Zero downtime, ensuring inquiries are answered instantly.
  • Multi-format Media Support: Instantly sends images, locations, and PDF brochures.
  • Smart CRM Integration: Pushes qualified lead data straight to the sales dashboard.
  • Seamless Human Handoff: Pauses the bot automatically when a user requests to speak to a human agent.

Results

Maximizing Sales Funnel Efficiency

90% Faster Response Time

Reduced initial inquiry response time from hours down to milliseconds.

300% Increase in Lead Capture

24/7 availability ensured night-time and weekend leads were captured immediately.

Massive Time Savings

Saved the sales team over 40 hours a week by eliminating repetitive manual replies.

"Implementing WhatsApp Automation through Dynamic Labz has enhanced our customer communication tremendously. Fast, reliable, and effective. A must-have for any business."

Akshat Shah Founder of Sanki Events

JumpinGo

High-Volume Event Ticketing & Booking Portal

The Goal

JumpinGo, a traveling jumping park festival, experiences massive bursts of website visitors the exact moment tickets go on sale for a new city. Their biggest challenge was keeping the website online during these intense traffic spikes while ensuring families could easily book specific time slots without the system double-booking.

How We Built It

Instead of relying on standard website templates that crack under pressure, we built a custom, highly reliable booking system designed specifically to handle thousands of simultaneous users. We streamlined the checkout process, making it incredibly fast for parents to select a date, pick a time slot, and securely pay for their tickets in just a few clicks.

Behind the scenes, we created a smart calendar system that updates ticket availability instantly for everyone browsing, completely eliminating the risk of overselling a time slot.

The Launch

During the first major ticket drop on the new platform, the system flawlessly handled the massive surge of excited customers. Thousands of tickets were processed in minutes with zero website crashes or slowdowns, resulting in a completely sold-out event and a stress-free experience for the business owners.

Benefits

Why this works for Event Organizers

Crash-Proof Reliability

The website stays online and fast, even when thousands of people try to buy tickets at the exact same second.

Lightning-Fast Checkout

A simple, straightforward buying process that gets customers from selecting a time to paying in under a minute.

Accurate Availability

Inventory updates instantly, so you never have to deal with the headache of refunding overbooked time slots.

Automated Ticketing

Customers receive their digital tickets and receipts instantly via email, completely hands-off for your team.

Property Management

Enhancing In-House Property Selection & Visit Planning

Problem Statement

Enhancing In-House Property Selection Process

The client, a leading property broker, faced significant challenges in managing property recommendations for walk-in clients. Their existing process was entirely manual, requiring employees to verbally gather client requirements and manually search through property records. This not only consumed valuable time but also led to inconsistencies and errors in capturing client preferences.

Additionally, the manual system made it difficult to plan property visits effectively. Coordinating schedules between clients, property owners, and brokers required extensive back-and-forth communication, causing delays and confusion. The client needed a comprehensive solution to streamline these processes, improve data accuracy, and enhance the overall client experience.

Our Solution

Property Listing & Visit Planning Portal

We developed an in-depth property listing portal tailored for the client's in-house operations. This portal streamlines the entire property selection process, starting with an extensive client requirement form comprising over 35+ questions. Once submitted, the system applies intelligent filters based on primary criteria—such as identifying properties within a 2.5 km radius.

The portal then presents the top 15 property matches with a visually appealing UI/UX design. Clients can shortlist properties they find interesting, moving seamlessly to the visit planner. Notifications are automatically sent to property owners, ensuring smooth coordination.

SECTION 01

Property Inquiry Form

A dynamic, user-friendly form capturing detailed client preferences effortlessly.

SECTION 02

Property Match Results

A visually appealing dashboard showcasing top property matches with detailed insights.

SECTION 03

Visit Planner

Simplified interface for scheduling property visits, enhancing client convenience and engagement.

Key Features

  • Advanced Client Requirement Form: Over 35+ detailed fields capturing precise preferences.
  • Intelligent Matching Algorithm: Prioritizes top 15 properties based on location and parameters.
  • Seamless Visit Planning: Effortless scheduling with automated notifications to owners.
  • Robust Management Module: Simplified property addition with over 70+ data points and RBAC.
